Orbi Chat AI · Effective November 17, 2026

Privacy Policy

This policy covers Orbi Chat AI, published by Nabeel Dwekat (angmon.io). Contact support@angmon.io for privacy and support requests.

On-device processing

Orbi uses Google DeepMind’s Gemma 4 E2B model. Google DeepMind developed the model. Orbi’s publisher operates the app and handles its data and reports. The app runs inference on your device. Prompts, replies, picked photos, recordings, settings, and message history are held in local app storage. Orbi opens the Android photo picker when you choose a photo and uses the microphone when you start a recording.

Downloads, purchases and voluntary sharing

The initial model download connects to Hugging Face, which receives the usual connection information to deliver the file. Google Play handles checkout, transaction information, and subscription management under Google’s Privacy Policy. Orbi uses subscription status to apply your plan. If you share an illustration through Android, your chosen destination receives that illustration.

Optional content reports

When you select a generated response and confirm sending a report, Orbi sends that reply or generated illustration, your selected reason, a random report reference, and the app version over HTTPS to its Cloudflare-hosted report service. Orbi does not attach your original prompts, uploaded photos, recordings, or conversation history. Check the selected reply for any private details it may contain before sending. Reports are reviewed for content safety, are limited to authorized operators, and expire from report storage after 90 days. Cloudflare processes connection information to operate and protect the service. We do not use the reports for advertising or sell them.

Deletion and privacy requests

Delete a chat from history to remove its stored messages and attachments, or clear app storage in Android settings. For an access or deletion request about a submitted report, email support with its reference or the approximate submission time and reason. Orbi does not require an account.

Using AI responses

Orbi is an experimental AI tool. Its output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable. Verify important information and use your own judgment before acting. Responses are general information and do not replace advice from a qualified medical, legal, financial, or other professional. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.
